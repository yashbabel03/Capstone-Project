SOP-EQ-011 Fryer Oil Quality and Change
Document type: equipment_manual
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2025-11-02
Review date: 2026-11-02
Applicable roles: staff, chef, manager, admin
Department: kitchen
Classification: equipment_manual

Section 1. Daily
Filter oil at close. Record TPM (total polar materials). Discard if TPM ≥ 24% or if smoke, foam, or fishy odor is present.

Section 2. Change procedure
1. Cool oil below 40°C before draining. Wear heat gloves and apron.
2. Drain into labeled waste caddies. Never pour oil into floor sinks.
3. Wipe kettle; do not hose water into a hot vat.
4. Refill with approved frying oil to the etched fill line.
5. Log vat id, TPM before, TPM after, and oil lot.

Section 3. Faults
If the fryer will not ignite or the high-limit trips, take the vat out of service, tag it, and open an incident assigned to Facilities. Do not bypass the high-limit switch.
