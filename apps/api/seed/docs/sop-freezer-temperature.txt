SOP-FS-014 Walk-in Freezer Temperature Excursion
Document type: SOP
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Review date: 2026-12-01
Applicable roles: staff, chef, manager, admin
Department: kitchen, prep, receiving
Classification: food_safety

Section 1. Purpose
Prevent unsafe product storage when a walk-in freezer drifts above the authorized temperature band. Authorized holding temperature is -18°C (0°F) or colder. An excursion is any reading warmer than -15°C (5°F) lasting 15 minutes or more, or any reading warmer than -12°C (10°F) immediately.

Section 2. Immediate actions (first 10 minutes)
1. Do not discard product yet. Do not power-cycle the freezer unless directed by a manager or technician.
2. Confirm the reading on the primary digital probe and a secondary calibrated thermometer in the warmest product (door-side top shelf).
3. Close the door. Stop non-essential entries. Place a "TEMP HOLD — DO NOT ENTER" magnet on the door.
4. Record time, displayed temperature, secondary probe temperature, and which sensor alarmed.
5. If temperature is warmer than -12°C, page the on-duty manager immediately and begin product assessment (Section 4).
6. If temperature is between -15°C and -12°C, monitor every 5 minutes. If it does not recover to -18°C within 30 minutes, treat as a full excursion.

Section 3. Equipment checks
1. Confirm door gasket seal, interior lights off, evaporator fan running, condenser intake not blocked.
2. Check circuit breaker FZ-1. Do not reset a tripped breaker more than once.
3. If ice is bridging the evaporator, do not chip ice. Report to facilities as "defrost failure".
4. Photograph the controller display and the secondary probe.

Section 4. Product disposition
Use time/temperature, not appearance alone.
- Product that remained at or below -12°C: retain, mark "EXC-HOLD", use first.
- Potentially hazardous ready-to-eat that exceeded -12°C for more than 2 hours: discard. Complete waste log.
- Raw protein that thawed (ice crystals gone, limp texture): do not refreeze for service. Discard or cook immediately only if a manager authorizes and a cook-through log is opened.
- Never taste product to decide safety.

Section 5. Escalation
Escalate to Manager if: temperature warmer than -12°C, breaker trips twice, coolant leak odor, or any discarded value over local threshold.
Escalate to Facilities if: compressor silent, alarms continue after door is sealed, or defrost failure is suspected.
If both Manager and Facilities are unreachable within 20 minutes, call the after-hours vendor list posted on the pass.

Section 6. Documentation
Create an incident in RestaurantOps with equipment id WALK-IN-FZ-01, start time, peak temperature, duration, product actions, and photos. Manager must verify before the incident is closed. Update this SOP's review if the root cause is a procedure gap.
