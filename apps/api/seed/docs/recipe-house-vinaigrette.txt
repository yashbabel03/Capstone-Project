RCP-GAR-003 House Red Wine Vinaigrette
Document type: recipe
Version: 1.3
Effective date: 2025-09-01
Review date: 2026-09-01
Applicable roles: chef, manager, admin
Department: prep
Classification: recipe

Yield: 2 liters
Ingredients: 600 ml red wine vinegar, 1.2 L extra virgin olive oil, 40 g Dijon, 20 g kosher salt, 8 g black pepper, 30 g minced shallot.
Method: Whisk vinegar, Dijon, salt. Stream oil. Fold shallot last. Hold at 1–4°C, 5-day discard.
Allergens: none of the priority eight if Dijon lot is gluten-tested. Confirm lot on the allergen matrix.
Restricted: recipe details are Chef and above.
