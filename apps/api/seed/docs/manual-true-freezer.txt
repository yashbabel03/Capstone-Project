Section 4.2 Alarm codes
E1: probe fault. Replace cabinet probe; do not ignore.
E2: high temperature. Default alarm at -12°C. Silence does not clear the fault.
E3: door open > 3 minutes.

Section 4.3 Setpoint
Factory setpoint -20°C. Do not raise warmer than -18°C without Manager approval.

Section 7. Defrost
Electric defrost every 6 hours for 20 minutes. If coil remains iced after two cycles, call authorized service. Do not puncture the coil.
